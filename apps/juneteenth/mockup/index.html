Juneteenth

A show for the 72 × 16 LED display that opens solemn and becomes joyful, the way the day is observed: a quiet dawn over Galveston Bay, the news arriving on June 19, 1865, the Juneteenth flag going up, a family cookout, red drinks and red foods, music in the park, and fireworks. Between the scenes come a clock and a countdown to June 19, and on the day itself JUNETEENTH and FREEDOM DAY. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    On June 19, 1865, Union troops arrived in Galveston, Texas, and announced General Order No. 3: the enslaved people there learned they were free, more than two years after the Emancipation Proclamation. Juneteenth has been a federal holiday since 2021.

    Seven scenes. It begins quietly: dawn over the bay, a lighthouse and a sailing ship; then a rider brings the news and posts a notice, people gather and raise their arms, and the date appears. Then the celebration: the Juneteenth flag goes up (a white star in a white nova, on blue over red, split by an arc for a new horizon), a family cookout, red drinks and red foods (red stands for resilience), music and dancing in the park, and fireworks with HAPPY JUNETEENTH and FREEDOM DAY.

    No likenesses of real people and no images of suffering: the solemn part is about dawn, news and hope. People are simple faceless figures with a range of warm skin tones. The art is original.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to June 19. On the day it becomes JUNETEENTH, FREEDOM DAY and HAPPY JUNETEENTH. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      Key decisions

      • D3Solemn, then joyful. Dawn and the news first; the flag, food, music and fireworks after.
      • D4Hope, not trauma. No chains, violence or likenesses: a rider, a notice, raised arms.
      • D5The flag, drawn right. Star in a nova, centered; an arc horizon, blue over red. It waves by whole rows.
      • D6Faceless figures. A range of warm skin tones, light clothes, never dark pixels for features.
      • D8The day's cards. JUNETEENTH, FREEDOM DAY / JUNE 19, HAPPY / JUNETEENTH, CELEBRATE / FREEDOM.
      • D10START previews the day. A new cycle starts on a count card, so the change shows at once.
