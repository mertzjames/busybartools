Advent Calendar

An Advent calendar for the BUSY Bar's 72 × 16 LED display. Each day a door opens on a new little animation, from the first snowflake on December 1 to Christmas morning, then a card counts down to Christmas using the bar's own clock. This page plays the same frames the bar plays, at 15 frames per second.

The calendar

  • Every loop opens the day's door, plays that day's vignette, then shows the countdown card.
  • December 1 to 23: the card reads N DAYS / TO CHRISTMAS.
  • Christmas Eve: hours and minutes until midnight, HH:MM / TO GO, ticking each minute.
  • Christmas Day: the finale, then MERRY / CHRISTMAS.
  • Before December: a winter scene and N DAYS / TO ADVENT. From December 26: the finale and SEE YOU / NEXT YEAR!, with all 25 doors open to browse.

Behind every door

Key moments from each day's vignette. They grow busier toward Christmas: quiet objects in the first week, animals and treats next, then characters, and finally the tree, the sleigh and the star. Click a frame to show it on the simulated bar.
