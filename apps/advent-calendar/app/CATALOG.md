A different animation behind every door of Advent, with a live countdown to Christmas.

Each loop, a door on the calendar swings open onto today's date, that day's vignette plays, and a card counts down to Christmas from your bar's own clock. On Christmas Eve the card counts the hours and minutes to midnight, and on Christmas Day the finale plays with a MERRY CHRISTMAS.

## Features

- **25 original vignettes** from December 1 to the Christmas Day finale: first snow, hot cocoa, a penguin on the ice, gingerbread friends, carolers, a nutcracker, a sleigh ride across the moon and more. They get busier as Christmas gets closer.
- **Smooth full-screen animation** at 15 fps, played by the firmware.
- **Live countdown** to Advent, to Christmas, then to midnight on Christmas Eve, all from the bar's local time.
- **Revisit any door** you have already opened with the encoder.
- Before Advent, a snowy cabin scene counts down the days until the first door opens.

## Controls

- **Encoder:** browse the doors opened so far (all 25 after Christmas). Turn back onto today to follow today again.
- **OK:** back to today.
- **Back:** quit.

## About

Original pixel art, part of the open-source [busybartools](https://github.com/mertzjames/busybartools) collection (MIT license). The source, a browser preview and the full list of vignettes are in the [app's folder](https://github.com/mertzjames/busybartools/tree/main/apps/advent-calendar). The app sends no data anywhere.
