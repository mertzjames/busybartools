Mother’s Day

Flowers, hugs and breakfast in bed for Mother’s Day on a 72 × 16 LED display: a bouquet gathered stem by stem, breakfast carried in on a tray, a parent and child growing a garden, a handmade card that opens to MOM, a hug under a blooming tree, tea in a sunny window and a finale of hearts and flowers. Between them come a clock and a countdown, and on the day itself a greeting. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    Seven scenes, warm and sweet. A bouquet of tulips, roses, daisies and lavender gathers stem by stem and is tied with a ribbon. A child carries breakfast in bed. A parent and child plant a garden that blooms behind them. A crayon card opens to a heart and MOM. A child and a grandmother meet in a hug under the blossoms. Tea steams in a sunny window. And the finale says HAPPY MOTHER’S DAY with hearts and petals.

    Moms come in many forms, so the figures are generic: plain faces, a range of skin tones and hair, a mom, a grandmother, a parent and child, with no stereotypes beyond the warmth of the day.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to Mother’s Day (the second Sunday of May). On the day it becomes HAPPY MOTHER’S DAY and a few sweet words. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      Key decisions

      • D2Warm and inclusive. Generic figures with plain faces and a range of skin tones; moms, grandmothers and parents.
      • D3A day in seven scenes. Flowers first, then breakfast, the garden, the card, a hug, tea, and a finale.
      • D4Lit backdrops for people. A pillow behind Mom, a sunset glow behind the hug, so faces and hair read.
      • D5Steady light. Glows and sunbeams hold still; only small things move, so the files stay small.
      • D7Two-line cards. MOTHER’S DAY fits the big font only edge to edge, so the cards put it in the small one.
      • D8START previews the day. The cycle restarts on a card, so the change shows at once.
