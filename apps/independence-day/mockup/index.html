Independence Day

The Fourth of July on a 72 × 16 LED display: a hometown parade, the Liberty Bell ringing, a backyard cookout, an eagle soaring past a waving flag, sparklers at dusk, fireworks over the city and a finale. Between them come a clock and a countdown to the Fourth, and on the day itself a count of the hours and minutes until the fireworks at dusk. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    Seven scenes follow one Fourth of July from morning to night. A marching band and a float roll down Main Street while kids wave little flags, the Liberty Bell swings and rings (crack and all), burgers sizzle on a backyard grill and a kid makes off with a slice of watermelon, an eagle circles past a waving flag, sparklers write USA in the dusk, and fireworks fill the sky over the city before the finale spells out the greeting.

    Festive and family-friendly, and non-partisan: no likenesses of real people and no official seals. The flag is drawn correctly: thirteen one-row stripes, red at the top and bottom, and a blue canton over the top seven with the stars as a dense checker of dots.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to July 4th. On the Fourth it becomes HAPPY 4TH OF JULY and FIREWORKS IN with the hours and minutes left until dusk (21:00 on the bar's clock), then ENJOY THE FIREWORKS! once dusk comes. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date; press it again for a dusk demo that sets the cards to 20:58 on the Fourth, so the countdown runs out in a minute or two; a third press goes back.
