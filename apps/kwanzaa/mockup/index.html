Kwanzaa

The seven days of Kwanzaa, December 26 to January 1, on a 72 × 16 LED display. Each day the kinara lights that day's candles, a card answers Habari gani? with the principle of the day, and scenes of the harvest, the unity cup, drums and dance, a woven cloth and the karamu feast play between a clock and a countdown. The simulator plays the same frames the bar plays, at 15 frames per second, with your current time and date.

    The show

    Every cycle opens at the Kwanzaa table: the mkeka (mat), the mazao (crops) and muhindi (corn), the kikombe cha umoja (unity cup) and the kinara. A taper lights the day's candles in the order the official Kwanzaa website gives: the black candle first, then the red and the green candles alternately, each side from left to right. Before December 26 the candles wait, unlit.

    Between the scenes, a clock card shows the time and date, and a count card counts the days to Kwanzaa. During Kwanzaa the count card greets you with HABARI GANI? ("What's the news?") and answers with the day's principle, in Swahili and English.

    Use START (or the menu) to preview each day of Kwanzaa on any date, as the bar's START button does.

    Families light the candles in different orders, so the bar has a Candle order setting (Apps → Kwanzaa → Setup): Official site (the default, described above) or From the center, which lights the red next to the black candle on day 2, the green next to it on day 3, then the middle pair, then the outer pair. The candle-order menu above shows either.

      Key decisions

      • D3The official lighting order. Black first, then red and green alternately, each side left to right (officialkwanzaawebsite.org).
      • D4Eight kinara renders. Unlit, then one per day; the app picks the day's from the bar's date.
      • D5A lit black candle. Black is an unlit LED, so the black candle is charcoal grey with a highlight.
      • D6Habari gani? The greeting, then its answer: the day's principle in Swahili over the English.
      • D7Holiday mode on START. Each press previews the next day, 1 to 7, then returns to the real date.
      • D14Two candle orders. A bar setting picks the official site's order or center-out; six extra kinara renders.
      • D8Simple figures, warm skin tones. No eyes on 3-pixel faces, no caricature; headwraps and kufis in bright colors.
