Veterans Day

A tribute for Veterans Day, November 11, which honors everyone who served in the U.S. armed forces, living and dead, on a 72 × 16 LED display. Calm scenes of flags along Main Street, a parade, a salute at sunrise, a field of poppies, a child's thank-you and a closing flag. Between them come a clock and a countdown, on the day a few words of thanks, and at 11:00 on November 11 a quiet card for the 11th hour. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The tribute

    Six calm scenes. The town puts a flag out on every lamppost along Main Street at sunrise; veterans come down the street in a parade, with a flag bearer, three veterans marching in step and two older veterans waving from an open car, while neighbours wave small flags; a service member and an older veteran salute as the flag goes up; red poppies open across a field; a child hands an older veteran a handmade card; and the flag fills the display behind THANK YOU VETERANS.

    The 11th hour. The armistice that ended the First World War took effect at 11:00 on November 11, 1918, and Veterans Day began as Armistice Day. At 11:00 on November 11 (the bar's local time) the app starts a new cycle with a quiet card: 11TH HOUR, 11TH DAY / 11TH MONTH, ARMISTICE / NOV 11 1918 and THANK YOU / VETERANS. Then the show resumes.

    No insignia, seals, branch emblems or medals: plain uniforms with no patches, flags and poppies. No likenesses of real people and nothing that depicts combat or weapons. Veterans of different ages, skin tones and genders.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to Veterans Day. On the day it becomes VETERANS DAY and a few words of thanks. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview: the first press shows the day, the second opens every cycle with the 11:00 moment, the third goes back.

      • D2A tribute, calm and warm. Gratitude, not a party: no fireworks, confetti or jokes.
      • D3No insignia, no likenesses, no combat. Plain uniforms, flags, poppies; a range of veterans.
      • D6The 11th hour. At 11:00 on Nov 11 a new cycle opens with the moment card.
      • D7Words. VETERANS DAY, THANK YOU VETERANS, HONOR ALL WHO SERVED, THANK YOU FOR YOUR SERVICE.
