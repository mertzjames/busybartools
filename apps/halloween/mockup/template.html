Halloween

A spooky-fun Halloween show for a 72 × 16 LED display: jack-o'-lanterns carved and lit, a haunted house, trick-or-treaters, bats across the moon, a friendly ghost, a black cat on a fence, a witch on her broom and a skeleton dance. Between them come a clock and a countdown in nights, and on the night itself a greeting and spooky phrases. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    Eight scenes, spooky but family-friendly. Four pumpkins are carved one at a time, each with its own face, and lit. Lightning flickers behind a haunted house, which lights up window by window; a shadow passes the green window and the door creaks open. Trick-or-treaters in a sheet ghost, a witch hat, a pumpkin suit and a cape collect candy. Bats stream across the moon. A friendly ghost rises from a graveyard and says BOO! A black cat arches its back on a fence. A witch flies past the moon on her broom. Three skeletons dance to the beat, and Halloween confetti closes the show.

    The colors are glowing pumpkin orange, slime green and purple over a deep violet night. Black things (the cat, the bats, the witch's dress) are lit violet-greys with a rim light, since black is an unlit LED.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date, by candlelight under cobwebs. A countdown card shows the nights to Halloween (October 31). On the night it becomes HAPPY HALLOWEEN and phrases like TRICK OR TREAT! and BOO! Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      Key decisions

      • D2Spooky-fun, never scary. Friendly faces, no gore; generic costumes and original characters.
      • D3Eight scenes. Carving Night opens; the Skeleton Dance with confetti closes.
      • D5Black is unlit. The cat, bats and witch's dress are lit violet-greys with rim light and lit eyes.
      • D7Nights, not days. 30 NIGHTS / TO HALLOWEEN; cobwebs, a candle and little lanterns frame the cards.
      • D8One source of truth. The app, mockup and tests share app/shared/halloween.ts.
      • D9START previews the night. The cycle restarts on a card, so the change shows at once.
