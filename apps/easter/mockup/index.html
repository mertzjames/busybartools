Happy Easter

A springtime show for the 72 × 16 LED display: a bunny hiding eggs in the garden, an egg hunt, chicks hatching, eggs being painted, spring flowers opening, an Easter basket and a sunrise. Between the scenes come a clock and a countdown to Easter Sunday, and on the day itself HAPPY EASTER. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    Seven cheerful scenes. The bunny hops through a sunny garden and hides three eggs. Two kids hunt for them and fill a basket. Three pastel eggs crack open and chicks pop out. A brush paints white eggs with zigzags, dots and stripes. Tulips and daffodils open in the sun while butterflies arrive. Eggs and a chocolate bunny fill an Easter basket. The finale is a sunrise over spring hills, with Easter lilies opening and HAPPY EASTER in the sky.

    Pastels stay lit and saturated so they read on the LEDs, and every small feature (eyes, cracks) is a lit color. The art is original: a generic cartoon bunny, chicks and eggs.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to Easter Sunday (Western Easter, which moves between March 22 and April 25). On the day it becomes HAPPY EASTER and a cheerful extra page. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      Key decisions

      • D2The cheerful side of Easter. Bunny, eggs, chicks and flowers, for every family.
      • D3One calm traditional note. The finale is a sunrise with Easter lilies; no cross.
      • D4Pastels that stay lit. Saturated pastels on deep backdrops; blue eggs never sit on blue sky.
      • D5Lit small features. Plum and brown eyes, a plum crack line; kids are faceless profiles.
      • D8The day's cards. HAPPY / EASTER, then EGG HUNT TIME!, HOPPY EASTER! or SPRING IS HERE!
      • D9START previews the day. The cycle restarts on a card, so the change shows at once.
