Labor Day

A tribute to working people that opens into the last long weekend of summer, on a 72 × 16 LED display: ironworkers topping out a building at sunrise, a parade of trades, the harvest, a backyard cookout, a last swim at sunset and a fireworks finale. Between them come a clock and a countdown, and on the day itself a greeting. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    Six scenes move through one day, from sunrise to fireworks. First the work: a crane lifts the last beam of a new building, painted white with a flag and a little evergreen on it (the ironworkers' topping-out tradition), and a welder sets it in a shower of sparks. A nurse, a teacher, a farmer, a builder, a mechanic and a chef march down Main Street, and a red tractor bales hay in the golden hour. Then the day off: burgers on the grill at a backyard cookout, one last swim as the sun sets on summer, and a crowd of workers in hard hats cheering the fireworks over the finished tower.

    Warm and non-partisan: generic workers in a range of skin tones, plain faces, no real people, and no union or company logos. The only words in the scenes are the finale's THANK YOU and WORKERS.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to Labor Day (the first Monday of September). On the day it becomes HAPPY LABOR DAY and a short phrase. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      Key decisions

      • D2A tribute, then a day off. Generic workers, a range of skin tones, plain faces; no real people, no logos.
      • D3Sunrise to fireworks. Three scenes of work, then the cookout, the beach and the finale.
      • D4Topping out. The last beam goes up white, with a flag and an evergreen, as ironworkers do it.
      • D5Trades you can read. Each worker is told by a hat and a tool: cap, bun, straw hat, hard hat, ball cap, toque.
      • D7Two big lines. TO LABOR DAY fits the big font (67 px), so every card uses it.
      • D8START previews the day. The cycle restarts on a card, so the change shows at once.
