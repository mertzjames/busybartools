Father’s Day

A summer day with Dad on a 72 × 16 LED display: fishing at dawn, playing catch, a first bike ride, burgers on the grill, a gift with a gloriously wild tie, a hammock nap at sunset and fireworks to finish. Between them come a clock and a countdown, and on the day itself a greeting. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    Seven scenes move through one summer day, from sunrise to fireworks. A parent and child catch (and let go) a little sunfish off the dock, toss a ball across the lawn, fix a wobbly bike wheel and ride off, and a dad flips burgers onto a waiting bun. A present pops open to a necktie that can't make up its mind, a granddad naps in a hammock as a leaf lands on his belly, and the night ends with fireworks, a trophy and #1 DAD.

    Warm, lighthearted and for everyone: dads, granddads and father figures with a range of skin tones and hair, and gentle humor that doesn't put anyone down. The only words in the scenes are DAD on the gift tag, TA-DA!, the Z's of the nap and the finale's greeting.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to Father’s Day (the third Sunday of June). On the day it becomes HAPPY FATHER’S DAY and a short phrase. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      Key decisions

      • D2Warm and inclusive. Dads, granddads and father figures; many skin tones; gentle jokes only.
      • D3Dawn to night. Fishing at sunrise first; fireworks close the show.
      • D4Readable people. Plain faces, lit hair, and a dark band or plain sky behind every figure.
      • D5#1 DAD. The pixel fonts have no #, so the app adds one locally.
      • D7Big words on the day. FATHER'S DAY fits the big font, so the greeting is two big lines.
      • D8START previews the day. The cycle restarts on a card, so the change shows at once.
