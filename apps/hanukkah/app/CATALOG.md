A Hanukkah show built around a menorah that follows tonight's candle count, with a countdown to Hanukkah the rest of the year.

Each evening at candle time the shamash is lit and lights tonight's candles, one more each night for eight nights. Around the menorah play a spinning dreidel, latkes sizzling in the pan, sufganiyot under powdered sugar, a cascade of gelt, a menorah glowing in a snowy window and a blue-and-silver finale.

## Features

- **Tonight's menorah:** the candles stand in the rightmost cups and the shamash lights them left to right. The dates come from the Hebrew calendar, so the app never needs a yearly update.
- **Candle time at sunset:** each night's new candle appears at that day's sunset, worked out from the bar's location, or estimated from its time zone if it has none.
- **Seven animated scenes** at 15 fps, played by the firmware.
- **Clock and countdown cards** between scenes: the time and date, the days to Hanukkah, and during the holiday the night's greeting (NIGHT 3, HAPPY HANUKKAH, CHAG SAMEACH).
- Outside Hanukkah the menorah rests unlit and the finale is left out.

## Settings

- **Candle time:** Sunset (the default), or a fixed time from 16:00 to 18:00.
- **Location:** used for sunset times. Without coordinates, sunset is estimated from the bar's time zone.

## Controls

- **OK:** skip to the next scene or card.
- **Encoder:** step forward or back through the show.
- **START:** holiday mode, to preview night 1, then 2, up to 8 on any date. A ninth press goes back to today's show.
- **Back:** quit.

## About

Original pixel art, part of the open-source [busybartools](https://github.com/mertzjames/busybartools) collection (MIT license). The source and a browser preview of every night are in the [app's folder](https://github.com/mertzjames/busybartools/tree/main/apps/hanukkah). The app sends no data anywhere.
