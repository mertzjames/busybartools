Happy Hanukkah

A menorah for a 72 × 16 LED display that follows tonight's candle count, with a dreidel, latkes, sufganiyot, gelt, a snowy window and a blue-and-silver finale. Between scenes a clock card and a countdown card appear; during Hanukkah the countdown becomes the night's greeting. The simulator plays the same frames the bar plays, at 15 frames per second, picked for your current date and time.

    The show

    The heart of the show is tonight's menorah. Candles are placed from right to left, one more each night, and lit from left to right, newest first. The shamash, raised in the middle, is lit first and then lights the others. The bar has no sunset table, so 17:00 stands in for sundown: on the first evening the first candle appears at 17:00, each later evening adds one, and all eight stay lit through the last day. Outside Hanukkah the menorah rests, unlit.

    The count card says 18 DAYS / TO HANUKKAH before, TOMORROW and then TONIGHT / HANUKKAH BEGINS, and during Hanukkah NIGHT 3 / HAPPY HANUKKAH, then CHAG SAMEACH. The clock card shows the time, then the date.

    Holiday mode previews Hanukkah on any date: each press of START shows the next night, 1 to 8, and a ninth press goes back to today's show.

    Key decisions

    • D2Nine pre-rendered menorahs. The menorah and the window come in 0 to 8 candles, and the app picks tonight's.
    • D317:00 is sundown. A fixed hour stands in for a sunset table the bar doesn't have.
    • D3Right to left, then left to right. Candles are placed from the right and lit newest first, by the shamash.
    • D5Blue, silver and gold. Lit flames, no black details, warm and celebratory.
    • D7START previews the nights. Night 1 to 8, then back to today.
    • D9The finale only during Hanukkah. Outside it the show is a calm countdown.
