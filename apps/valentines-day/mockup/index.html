Valentine's Day

A sweet Valentine's show for a 72 × 16 LED display: rising hearts, a little cherub with a bow, a winged love letter, two lovebirds on a wire, a box of chocolates, a rose in bloom and a candy-heart finale with heart fireworks. Between them come a clock and a countdown, and on the day itself a greeting and candy-heart phrases. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    Seven scenes, sweet and family-friendly. Hearts rise until a big one beats in the middle. A cherub draws its bow, and the arrow bursts into a heart. A letter with little wings flutters in, opens, and says FOR YOU. Two birds hop along a wire until their beaks meet under a heart. A box of chocolates is opened and emptied one piece at a time. A rose grows and blooms. Candy hearts stack up for BE MINE, XOXO and heart fireworks.

    The colors are reds, pinks and warm whites with gold accents, over deep plum so the pinks stay bright.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to Valentine's Day (February 14). On the day it becomes HAPPY VALENTINE'S DAY and candy-heart phrases. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      Key decisions

      • D2Sweet, not romantic-explicit. Hearts, a cherub, birds, chocolates, a rose: family-friendly.
      • D3Seven scenes. Hearts Afloat opens; the candy-heart finale with heart fireworks closes.
      • D5Original art. A generic winged cherub; lit colors for eyes and small features.
      • D7Candy-heart cards. Two candy hearts frame the countdown and the phrases; the small line is gold.
      • D8One source of truth. The app, mockup and tests share app/shared/valentine.ts.
      • D9START previews the day. The cycle restarts on a card, so the change shows at once.
