Memorial Day

A quiet tribute for Memorial Day, which honors those who died in military service, on a 72 × 16 LED display. Slow scenes of the flag, a field of poppies, rows of white headstones, a lone bugler at sunset, a missing-man flyover and a memorial wall. Between them come a clock and a countdown, and on the day itself a few words. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The tribute

    Six slow scenes. The flag opens the show; poppies sway in a field; small flags stand before rows of plain white headstones as dusk falls; a lone bugler stands silhouetted against the setting sun; four aircraft fly over in formation and one climbs away alone, the missing man; and a wreath rests between two candles at a memorial wall, where the words WE REMEMBER appear.

    The flag follows the custom. On Memorial Day it flies at half-staff from the morning until noon, then is raised to full staff. The app picks the half-staff scene on the morning of the day and the full-staff scene from noon. On every other day the flag flies at full staff, its normal position.

    No insignia, seals or branch emblems, no likenesses of real people and nothing that depicts combat: flags, poppies, headstones, a silhouette and plain aircraft. Colors stay muted and the pace stays slow.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to Memorial Day (the last Monday of May). On the day it becomes MEMORIAL DAY and a few words. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date: the first press shows the morning (half-staff), the second the afternoon (full staff), the third goes back.

      Key decisions

      • D2A tribute, not a party. Slow pacing, muted color, no party effects, jokes, cookouts or sales.
      • D3No insignia, no likenesses, no combat. Flags, poppies, headstones, a silhouette, plain aircraft.
      • D5Half-staff until noon. Two flag scenes; the bar's clock picks one at the start of every cycle.
      • D6Full staff on other days. The flag's normal position.
      • D7Words. MEMORIAL DAY, WE REMEMBER, HONOR AND REMEMBER, and the flag's position.
      • D9START previews the day. Morning, afternoon, then back to the calendar.
