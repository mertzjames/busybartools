St. Patrick's Day

A festive St. Patrick's Day show for a 72 × 16 LED display, drawn from Irish folklore: a leprechaun dancing a jig, a rainbow ending at a pot of gold, a meadow of shamrocks with one lucky four-leaf clover, a parade with a piper and a drummer, a river dyed green, a Celtic knot that weaves itself, and green, white and orange fireworks. Between scenes come a clock and a countdown to March 17. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The show

    Seven scenes, about a minute and three quarters in all. A leprechaun in a buckled hat taps his foot on a hilltop at sunrise, then dances a jig and leaps for a heel click. A spring shower clears and a rainbow arcs to an old iron pot that overflows with gold. The view drifts over a meadow of shamrocks until one with four leaves catches the light. The parade marches past with the tricolor, a piper in a saffron kilt and a drummer, a work boat turns the river green, two cords weave a Celtic knot around ERIN GO BRAGH, and the finale is fireworks.

    It celebrates the day warmly, through folklore and heritage: no drinking jokes and no caricatures. The leprechaun is an original design, and the knot pattern is an original two-cord plait.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to St. Patrick's Day (March 17). On the day it becomes HAPPY ST. PATRICK'S DAY and ERIN GO BRAGH ("Ireland forever"). Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      • D2Festive and family-friendly. Folklore and heritage; no drinking jokes, no caricatures.
      • D3Seven scenes. The jig opens; fireworks close.
      • D5An original leprechaun. Green coat, buckled hat, ginger beard, lit blue eyes.
      • D6An original knot. A two-cord plait, over and under in turn.
