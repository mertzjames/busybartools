MLK Day

A quiet tribute for Martin Luther King Jr. Day on a 72 × 16 LED display. Slow scenes of dawn, a march of people of every age, a dove over the city, joined hands, ringing bells and a day of service. Between them come a clock and a countdown, and on the day itself a few words. The simulator below plays the same frames the bar plays, with your current time on the cards.

    The tribute

    Six slow scenes move from night into morning. A long line of people walks toward the sunrise, families hand in hand, a child on a parent's shoulders and an elder with a cane. A dove crosses a waking city. Neighbors raise joined hands, bells ring at first light, and a day of service plants a tree, fills a crate and paints a mural. The last scene is dawn on a mountaintop.

    There are no likenesses of real people and no quotations: figures are small and faceless, and each scene ends with a single word. Colors stay calm: deep blues and dawn golds.

    Between scenes, a clock card shows the time, then the date. A countdown card shows the days to MLK Day (the third Monday of January). On the day it becomes MLK DAY and a word. Press START (on the bar, or above) to preview the day on any date.

      Key decisions

      • D2A tribute, not a party. Slow pacing, calm color, no confetti or jokes, no likenesses, no quotations.
      • D3Night into morning. The march at sunrise opens; dawn on the mountaintop closes.
      • D5People of every skin tone and age. Small, faceless figures; every tone stays lit on the panel.
      • D6One word per scene. TOGETHER, PEACE, UNITY, FREEDOM, SERVICE, DREAM.
      • D7Calm cards. Warm white into dawn gold over a deep-blue shadow.
      • D9START previews the day. The cycle restarts on a card, so the change shows at once.
